About

A school built on discipline, care and steady standards.

What Maflun Academy stands for, and how it shows up in the classroom every day.

Maflun Academy sits on School Road in Malkali Farm, Mando, Kaduna — a school built to give children in the community a place where learning is taken seriously and every pupil is known by name.

We keep classes structured and teachers accountable, so that a child's progress is visible to parents at every stage, from nursery through to senior secondary school. Our motto, Brighter Future, guides everything we do.

01

Clear routines and standards of conduct that pupils carry beyond the school gate.

Character

Respect, honesty and responsibility, built into the culture of every classroom.

Academics

One school, three stages of growth.

A single, connected pathway from a child's earliest years through to their final school exams.

Ages 3–5

Nursery

  • Early literacy and numeracy
  • Guided play and social skills
  • Small class groups
Ages 6–11

Primary

  • Core subjects taught to the national curriculum
  • Regular tests and report cards
  • Sports, arts and clubs
Ages 12–17

Secondary

  • JSS and SSS preparation, including WAEC/NECO
  • Sciences, arts and commercial subjects
  • Exam preparation and career guidance
Admissions

How to enrol your child.

Admission is open across the school year, subject to space in each class.

1

Enquire

Call or email the school office with your child's age and the class you're applying for.

2

Visit and assess

Bring your child for a short entrance assessment and a tour of the school.

3

Register

Complete the registration form and submit your child's previous school records, if applicable.

4

Resume

Receive your child's class placement, resumption date and requirements list. The school office will set up your child's portal login.
